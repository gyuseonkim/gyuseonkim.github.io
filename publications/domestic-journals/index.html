  1. JKIISE
    전이학습기반 심층 강화학습 알고리즘을 활용한 동적 환경에서의 공간 적응적 자율이동 탐색 무인기 설계 (Design of a Deep Reinforcement Learning Algorithm for Spatially Adaptive UAV Autonomous Navigation based on Transfer Learning)
    Sungjoon Lee, Gyu Seon Kim, Taejin Woo, and 1 more author
    정보과학회논문지 (Journal of Korean Institute of Information Scientists and Engineers), February 2026
    Abs DOI Bib

    본 연구는 다양한 환경에서의 탐색 효율성을 향상시키기 위해 전이학습을 활용한 공간 적응적 무인기 자율비행을 위한 심층 강화학습 알고리즘을 제안한다. 무인기는 군사와 민간 임무에서 중요한 역할을 하며, 다양한 동적 환경에서 작동해야 하는 도전 과제를 안고 있다. 기존의 강화학습 방법은 새로운 환경에서 학습을 처음부터 다시 시작해야 하므로 비효율적이다. 이를 해결하기 위해, 본 연구에서는 전이학습을 활용하여 하나의 환경에서 학습한 지식을 다른 환경에 적용함으로써 학습 속도와 에너지 효율성을 향상시켰다. Deep Q-Networks (DQN)와 전이학습을 결합한 알고리즘을 통해 무인기는 다양한 임무 구역에서 효율적으로 탐색하고 적응할 수 있게 되었다. 실험 결과, 제안된 방법이 기존의 강화학습 기법에 비해 빠른 수렴 속도와 우수한 탐색 성능을 보여주었으며, 실전 응용 가능성을 확인하였다. This study proposes a deep reinforcement learning algorithm for spatially adaptive unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) autonomous navigation, utilizing transfer learning to enhance exploration efficiency across various environments. UAVs are vital for both military and civilian missions but face challenges when operating in diverse and dynamic settings. Traditional reinforcement learning methods are inefficient as they necessitate relearning from scratch in new environments. To overcome this limitation, the study implements transfer learning, which allows knowledge gained in one environment to be applied in another, thus improving learning speed and energy efficiency. By integrating Deep Q-Networks (DQN) with transfer learning, UAVs can effectively explore and adapt to different mission areas. Experimental results indicate that the proposed method achieves faster convergence and superior exploration performance compared to existing reinforcement learning techniques, highlighting its potential for practical applications.

    어뢰위협 회피를 위한 강화학습 기반 기만 전술 (Reinforcement Learning-Based Counter Measure Tactics to Avoid Torpedo Threat)
    Jaehyun Chung, Gyu Seon Kim, Soohyun Park, and 2 more authors
    한국통신학회논문지 (The Journal of Korean Institute of Communications and Information Sciences), March 2024
    한국통신학회 국내논문지 우수논문상 (Best Paper Award) Abs DOI Bib

    한국통신학회 국내논문지 우수논문상 (Best Paper Award)

    Research on future submarine systems is increasingly focused on integrated automation for intelligent mission support so that combat performance can be improved with advanced technology. One important component is the modernization of torpedo-countermeasure tactics as the capabilities of enemy torpedoes and decoys evolve. Because a torpedo usually travels far faster than a submarine, the threatened vessel must calculate an accurate response under severe time pressure. The paper formulates that decision as a reinforcement-learning problem and develops an algorithm that can derive an enemy-torpedo countermeasure for different situations while choosing the submarine’s evasive course. The learning process accounts for variables that can arise in the maritime environment, enabling the policy to adapt instead of following a fixed maneuver. The authors evaluate the algorithm’s effectiveness and use the results to establish its potential applicability to countermeasure tactics in real marine conditions.

    신뢰성 있는 지구 저궤도 위성 통신망 최적화를 위한 강화학습 기반 동적 라우팅 알고리즘 (Reinforcement Learning-Based Dynamic Routing for Robust Optimization of Low Earth Orbit (LEO) Satellite Communication Networks)
    Gyu Seon Kim, Soohyun Park, Joongheon Kim, and 3 more authors
    한국통신학회논문지 (The Journal of Korean Institute of Communications and Information Sciences), September 2023
    Abs DOI Bib

    The emergence of 6G has intensified interest in satellite systems that could deliver Internet access around the globe. Low-Earth-orbit satellites are particularly attractive for communications because they operate much closer to Earth than many other satellite platforms. Their high orbital velocity and the large number of spacecraft sharing an orbit, however, continually reshape network connectivity and make effective inter-satellite routing necessary. This study applies reinforcement learning to design a routing algorithm that optimizes communications across a dynamic LEO constellation. Several learning algorithms are evaluated on satellite topologies representative of conditions that may occur in space. The comparison is used both to assess the proposed approach’s performance and to show that reinforcement-learning-based routing can be deployed between satellites in an actual space environment.

  2. JKICS
    자율적인 UAM 시스템의 효율적인 무인 정보수집 및 감시를 위한 멀티 에이전트 기반 심층 강화학습 (Multi-Agent Deep Reinforcement Learning for Efficient Unattended Information Gathering and Monitoring of Autonomous UAM Systems)
    Chanyoung Park, Gyu Seon Kim, Kyeongjin Lee, and 1 more author
    한국통신학회논문지 (The Journal of Korean Institute of Communications and Information Sciences), February 2023
    Abs DOI Bib

    Multi-agent deep reinforcement learning allows a group of agents to communicate and cooperate toward one shared objective. The study uses this capability to replace part of the urban surveillance and information-collection role of fixed CCTV cameras with multiple Urban Air Mobility vehicles. Whereas a stationary camera offers only a limited view from one location, an autonomous UAM platform can move with a surveillance target and deliver flexible, dependable visual information in real time. The proposed system employs CommNet for communication among agents, enabling the UAM vehicles to coordinate their monitoring and gather information efficiently. In this way, the framework turns several mobile aerial vehicles into a cooperative, unattended urban observation system.
